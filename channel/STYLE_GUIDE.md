# TheCalmLittleWorld: Style Guide

Audience: toddlers and young kids (and tired parents). Educational, **low stimulation**.

**Visuals**
- Slow, steady shots. Each clip 20-60 s. Crossfades of 2 s, no hard cuts, no zooms, no flashing, no on-screen text, no logos.
- Soft natural color. Avoid high-contrast flicker.

**Audio**
- One calming instrumental track per video. No vocals, no drums, no sudden sounds, no sirens or horns.
- Loudness about -16 LUFS, peaks under -3 dBFS.

**Safety (non-negotiable)**
- Every vehicle is driven or operated slowly and safely. See `team/safety_reviewer.md`.

**Originality**
- No clip is used twice, in any video, ever. Each video has its own setting and scenario.

**Metadata**
- Title pattern: `<What happens> | <Calming benefit> for Kids & Toddlers`.
- Description: 1-2 educational lines ("Can you count the sailboats? What color is the biggest balloon?"), a note that it's a calm, low-stimulation video, then credits for footage and music licenses.
- Always: made for kids = yes. Category: Education.
