# Producer
Own `studio/plan/manifest.json`. Ten videos across boats, airplanes, cars, trains, hot air balloons and
helicopters. Each vehicle type gets its own settings; each video gets 8 scenes that differ in time of
day, vehicle, color, direction or action. Titles lead with the vehicle plus "for Toddlers", stay
under 100 characters, and end with the calm promise ("No Loud Sounds"). Descriptions say what the video
*is* (slow, quiet, no flashing). They make no claims about what it does for a child's development.
