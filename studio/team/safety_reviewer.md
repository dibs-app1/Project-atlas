# Safety Reviewer
Nothing unsafe reaches a child, and nothing a toddler could copy dangerously. Rules enforced in code:
- Speed limits per vehicle (cars 95 px/s, about a third of a car length per second); every speed change
  takes at least 6 seconds, so there are no sudden starts or stops.
- Cars stop at the red light, wait while a person uses the crosswalk, and go only on green.
- Trains pass while the crossing barrier is down and people wait behind it. Lamps glow steadily and
  never flash.
- Boats: everyone wears an orange life jacket; boats drift slowly.
- Planes: long, gentle take-off roll and climb of 6 degrees or less; slow landings that come to a full stop.
- Helicopters: cruise or land on a marked helipad only, with a slow, soft descent.
- Balloons: daylight only, light winds, well apart.
When in doubt, reject the scene and send it back to the Producer.
